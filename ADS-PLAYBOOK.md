# Money Move Homes: ads playbook (cost per click and cost per close)

Goal: a lower cost per closed deal than HomeLight, Zillow and the rest. We can't outbid them on head terms, and we don't need to. Lower cost per close comes from five levers.

## 1. Don't buy their expensive clicks. Buy the ones they ignore.
| Bucket | Example keywords | Why it's cheaper / better |
|---|---|---|
| New construction (DFW) | "new homes celina tx", "lennar princeton tx", "builder incentives frisco" | Referral sites barely bid here. Builder-paid fees, 30–60 day closes |
| Renters → owners | "rent vs buy dallas", "first time home buyer texas", "down payment assistance texas" | Informational terms are cheap ($1–3) and convert on our calculator |
| Spanish | "casas en venta dallas", "comprar casa con ITIN texas", "agente de bienes raices frisco" | Thin auction, underserved, fastest-growing buyer group |
| Relocation | "moving to dallas from california", "best dallas suburbs for families" | Out-of-state buyers need an agent. Mostly content clicks |
| Competitor alternatives | "homelight alternative", "homelight reviews", "is homelight legit" | High intent. **Do not use competitor trademarks in ad text.** Bidding on the keyword is allowed, but have an attorney approve it |
| AVOID | "realtor near me", "sell my house fast", "what's my home worth" | Most expensive (agents, investors and portals all bid) or junk leads |

## 2. Quality Score, a cheaper click on every keyword
- Every ad group lands on its exact page: Frisco keyword → `/agents/tx/frisco/`, new-build keyword → `/new-construction/`. With 374 city pages, every city gets its own landing page.
- Pages load fast (static, no frameworks) and work well on phones. Both feed Google's "landing page experience" score, which lowers your price per click.

## 3. Turn more visitors into leads (halves the cost per lead)
- Answer one question to start (the quick-start card), so no long form up front.
- Show the agent scorecard **before** asking for a phone number. Ask for the phone number last, with a "never sold" promise next to it.
- Benchmarks to beat: landing page conversion 8–12% (industry about 3–5%).

## 4. Turn more leads into closings (the biggest lever)
- A licensed Move Manager texts within 5 minutes during business hours (Clever's standard is about 5 minutes).
- One agent per lead, so the agent works it hard. Partners who don't update are cut.
- Nurture leads 6–12 months out with monthly market updates for their city (the city pages are the content).

## 5. Free traffic that compounds
- The renter list (Money Move Locating past clients): text and email them, $0 per click.
- SEO: 374 city pages, 6 "alternative" pages, the Texas buyer agreement guide. Expand to every US city with more than 10k people (about 3,500 pages) once the template proves it ranks.

## Math to beat (per close, about $2,400 fee to us)
| | Typical online lead | Money Move target |
|---|---|---|
| Cost per lead | $100–140 (LocaliQ avg.) | $25–45 |
| Lead → close | 2–4% | 5–8% (one agent, Move Manager, niche intent) |
| Cost per close | $2,500–7,000 | **$300–900** |

## Test plan (first $3,000)
1. $1,000: new construction, 6 DFW growth cities
2. $750: Spanish buyers, DFW
3. $750: renters → owners / first-time buyers
4. $500: "homelight alternative" and similar (only after attorney OK)
Track by campaign: cost per click → cost per lead → cost per appointment → cost per contract → cost per close. Cut any campaign above $60 per lead after 30 leads.
